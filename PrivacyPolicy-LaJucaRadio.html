Privacy Policy

App Name: La Juca Radio
Developer: Xatruch Tech
Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use La Juca Radio, an online radio application providing live audio streaming, song information, album artwork and recently played tracks.

1. Information We Collect

  • Connection information: Streaming and network infrastructure may process IP addresses, connection timestamps, request headers, listening duration and technical connection information to deliver audio and measure audience activity.
  • Device and usage information: Technical information such as device type, operating system, application errors and usage information may be processed where necessary to operate or troubleshoot the service.
  • Voluntarily provided information: If you contact us, we may receive your email address, name and the information you choose to include in your message.

Song titles, artist names, album artwork and station playback history describe the radio programming and are not necessarily a record of an individual listener's activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver live audio, including background playback.
  • Display current song information, artwork, playback notifications and recently played tracks.
  • Monitor streaming availability and audience statistics.
  • Diagnose technical problems, improve performance and protect the service.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers as necessary to operate the radio service, with your consent, or when required by law.

  • AzuraCast: Radio streaming management, station metadata and listener statistics.
  • Tailscale Funnel: Network infrastructure used to make the streaming service publicly accessible. See the Tailscale Privacy Policy.
  • Artwork and metadata services: Where external resources are requested, their providers may receive technical request information needed to deliver the content.

External websites and services may have their own privacy policies. Their processing practices are governed by their respective policies.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent where required by applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us to request its review or deletion.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information and maintain the security of our streaming infrastructure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for service operation, troubleshooting, audience measurement, support and applicable legal obligations. Retention by external providers may be subject to their own policies.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you. We will evaluate requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify your identity. Information that cannot reasonably be linked to an individual may not be identifiable for deletion.

7. Application Permissions

The application may use internet access, background media playback and media notifications to provide its radio functions. Available permissions and notification controls depend on your Android version and device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the application or its services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
